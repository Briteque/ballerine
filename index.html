Problem

Talent is global. Opportunity is not.

Systemic barriers block young athletes in emerging markets from ever reaching the starting grid – or the skating rink. We are focused on three structural gaps.

Infrastructure gap

Technical facilities are missing

Professional tracks, trampoline parks, and ice rinks are virtually non-existent in many African regions. Potential is stifled long before it can be developed.

Financial barrier

Capital-intensive sports, elitist outcomes

Motorsport and winter sports require significant capital. Without structured support, these disciplines remain the preserve of a narrow socioeconomic elite.

Representation void

No role models, no roadmap

Young athletes cannot become what they cannot see. The absence of relatable local champions creates a psychological ceiling on ambition.

Solution

Building the infrastructure of dreams.

OctWan is a multi-sport development platform that lowers the cost of entry, structures athlete progression, and connects talent to global pathways.

Democratise access

Multi-sport hubs

We build and manage hubs that combine karting, trampoline, and ice sports – shared infrastructure that makes premium sports accessible at scale.

Structured development

From curiosity to podium

Professional coaching curriculums take athletes from grassroots discovery through to high-performance programs, with clear milestones and standards.

Global pathways

Bridging to the world

Our hubs are plugged into international championships, federations, and professional teams, turning local output into global opportunities.

Model

A multi-sport engine for sustainable growth.

Grassroots accessibility paired with high-value professional pathways creates diversified revenues and a scalable operating model.

Motorsport (karting)

Gateway to global racing

Revenue from arrive-and-drive rentals, corporate leagues, and academy tuition – anchored in a standardised, safe, and professional karting ecosystem. The gateway to F1 and GT racing.

Winter sports

Ice skating & curling clinics

Active lifestyle

Trampoline parks & fitness hubs

High-volume recreational usage subsidises elite training programs, creating a virtuous cycle between fun, fitness and performance.
